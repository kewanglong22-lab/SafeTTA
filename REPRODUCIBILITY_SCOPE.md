# Reproducibility scope

## Level A — exact paper-statistic replay

Supported and validated with `PASS_NUMERIC_REPLAY`.

## Level B — broader frozen inference / full historical chain

Not all assets are included in the minimal public replay package. Historical
full-chain-only assets are intentionally excluded from the Level-A bundle.

## Level C — bit-identical upstream segmentation retraining

Not claimed. Complete historical per-image training manifests are not available
for every source segmentation state.
